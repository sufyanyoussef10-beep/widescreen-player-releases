# Widescreen Player

The music player of Widescreen (LED screen, light, sound).

## Download

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows | [Widescreen-Player-Setup.exe](https://github.com/sufyanyoussef10-beep/widescreen-player-releases/releases/latest/download/Widescreen-Player-Setup.exe) |
| Mac (Apple or Intel chip) | [Widescreen-Player-mac.dmg](https://github.com/sufyanyoussef10-beep/widescreen-player-releases/releases/latest/download/Widescreen-Player-mac.dmg) |

### Windows

1. Download **Widescreen-Player-Setup.exe** (link above) and open it.
2. The first time, Windows may show a blue box "Windows protected your PC", because the app is not registered with Microsoft: press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Press **Next**, then **Install**, then **Finish**. The player opens, and there is a **Widescreen Player** icon on the Desktop and in the Start menu.

No administrator password is needed. To remove it later: Settings, Apps, Widescreen Player, Uninstall (your songs and settings are kept).

### Mac

1. Open the `.dmg` file.
2. Drag **Widescreen Player** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. The first time only: the app is not registered with Apple, so macOS asks before it opens it.
   Open **Applications**, hold **Control** and click **Widescreen Player**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   On macOS 15 or newer, if there is no *Open* button: open **System Settings, Privacy & Security**, scroll down and press **Open Anyway**.

## Updates

You install it once. After that the player updates itself: when there is a new version, a green **UPDATE** button appears at the top of its window. Press it and the player closes and opens again with the new version. Your songs and settings stay as they are. If you do not press it, the new version is used the next time you open the player.

Still on version 1.1? That one cannot update itself yet. Install the current version once, the same way as the first time. Your songs and settings stay.

What is new in each version is listed on the [releases page](https://github.com/sufyanyoussef10-beep/widescreen-player-releases/releases).
